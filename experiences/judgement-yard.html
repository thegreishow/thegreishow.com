The experience

Reggae history that is still alive.

Judgement Yard sits in August Town, Kingston, in a lush valley setting connected to reggae music, Rastafari and community life. This guided visit is designed to give context—not just access—so you understand the people, stories and cultural significance behind what you see.

Depending on the day, your visit may include the natural pool, King Tafari Sound System, community interaction, a studio stop and other parts of the yard. Artist encounters and studio activity are never guaranteed and depend on availability.
