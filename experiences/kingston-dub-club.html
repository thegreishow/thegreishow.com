The experience

Kingston from the highest regions.

Kingston Dub Club is a hilltop roots-reggae institution on Skyline Drive in Jack's Hill. Sunday nights bring together selectors, artists, locals and travellers around sound-system culture, panoramic views and an atmosphere that has become part of Kingston's contemporary reggae story.

This is not just transport to a club. Your guide helps you understand the music, the language, the sound-system tradition and the social codes of the space so you can enter the night with confidence and context.
