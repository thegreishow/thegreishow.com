The Grei Show

The Grei Show is the creative identity of Gamel Reid — a Jamaican recording artist, producer, songwriter and multidisciplinary creator building music, visual art, literature, photography, technology and interactive experiences.

The story

Rooted in Jamaica. Built without borders.

Raised in Bull Bay and based in Kingston, Gamel Reid developed The Grei Show as more than a stage name. It became the meeting point for the performer, producer, writer, engineer, visual thinker and entrepreneur.

His musical language draws from reggae, dancehall and Jamaican sound-system culture while remaining open to hip-hop, electronic music, cinematic composition and experimental textures.

As a producer, he has contributed to records connected to Protoje, Blvk H3ro, Bay-C, Swnkah, Sugar Minott and other Jamaican artists. His work on Protoje’s “Weed & Ting,” co-produced with Supa Dups for In Search of Lost Time, placed his production within a major international reggae release.

Through Wheel It! Records, The Grei Show develops releases, collaborations and multimedia ideas independently, treating each project as part of a larger creative ecosystem.

“A song can become a film. A film can become a game. A character can become a book. The medium changes, but the world remains connected.”
